## Verified from the repository

- Nimbo is an open-source Kotlin Multiplatform weather application for Android and Apple platforms.
- The product presents current conditions, a chronological recent-to-future weather timeline, recent-weather comparisons, a 10-day forecast, air-quality context, cached weather, widgets, and companion watch surfaces.
- Approximate location is optional; city search works without location permission.
- The app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, subscription, or background-location permission in the reviewed code and declarations.
- Weather/place data comes from Open-Meteo, with GeoNames as the geocoding place-data basis. Historical values are model/reanalysis data, not verified observations from a specific station.
- “Best Time Outside” is deterministic product guidance based on weather conditions; it is not a safety, medical, or accuracy guarantee.

Product site: [nimbo.uz](https://nimbo.uz/).

Repository source: [github.com/4810092/Weather](https://github.com/4810092/Weather).
