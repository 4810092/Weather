### Nomination description

> Nimbo 1.1.0 makes the first forecast faster for people in Uzbekistan. On
> first launch, a user can choose Tashkent or one of six other major cities,
> use optional approximate location, or search for any city without granting
> location access. After the first useful forecast, one contextual tip points
> to saved places or the widget. Nimbo then keeps yesterday, now, and the next
> 24 hours on one timeline, provides a deterministic Best Time Outside window,
> and shows a 10-day forecast with UV and air-quality context. The September
> editorial angle is practical planning around late-summer heat and early
> autumn changes in Uzbekistan. The guidance is general weather context, not a
> medical, emergency-safety, or forecast-accuracy guarantee.
