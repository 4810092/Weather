# Nimbo press asset usage

These files are provided for factual editorial coverage of Nimbo Weather.

- Identify the product as **Nimbo Weather** and link to `https://nimbo.uz/` or
  the relevant public store page.
- Keep the Nimbo icon's colors, proportions, and artwork intact. Screenshots
  may be cropped for layout, but the visible application UI must not be edited.
- Do not imply endorsement, partnership, certification, or a live-weather value
  that Nimbo has not stated. Screenshot weather values are historical examples,
  not current conditions.
- Do not use the icon as another product's identity or redistribute the press
  files as a standalone asset pack.
- Verify current platform availability and version status on the public store
  pages before publication.

The repository's Apache-2.0 software license does not grant additional rights
to Nimbo product names or marks beyond reasonable descriptive use. Questions
can be raised through the public Nimbo support or GitHub Discussions links.
