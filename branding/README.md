# Nimbo brand assets

`source/nimbo-icon-master.png` is the opaque generated brand master. `source/nimbo-mark-chroma.png` is the generated extraction source used to produce the transparent Android adaptive foreground.

The identity represents recent, current, and future weather as three nested atmospheric horizons. It deliberately avoids literal sun/cloud clipart. Keep the mark centered, preserve its proportions, and retain generous padding for platform masks.

Production derivatives:

- Android adaptive and monochrome foreground: `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/nimbo_mark.png`
- iOS App Icon: `iosApp/Nimbo/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-1024.png`

The source images were generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow. Do not re-export them through a lossy format.
