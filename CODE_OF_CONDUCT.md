# Code of Conduct

Nimbo contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with respect for the privacy of everyone involved.
