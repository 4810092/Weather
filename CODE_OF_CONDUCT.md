# Code of Conduct

Nimbo contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate in issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person’s private information are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks and sustained disruption are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, explain the violated expectation when safe to do so, and protect the privacy of reporters and affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to Khasan Ganikhodjaev at `4810092@gmail.com`. Do not use a public issue. Security vulnerabilities should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
