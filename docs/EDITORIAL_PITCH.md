# Nimbo editorial pitch

## One-line story

Nimbo explains tomorrow through weather you already remember, then identifies the
safest comfortable time to be outside — privately, offline-first, and without an
account, advertising, or analytics.

## What is editorially distinctive

- A deterministic, explainable comparison with weather the user recently felt,
  instead of another generic number-heavy forecast.
- A 48-hour past-to-future timeline and a safety-gated Best Time Outside window.
- A 10-day outlook, air quality, and up to ten saved places without feature bloat.
- Home and lock-screen WidgetKit surfaces, an Android home widget, plus watchOS
  and Wear OS companions that keep the essential forecast glanceable.
- Thirteen complete locales including Uzbek and Arabic RTL, large-text layouts,
  screen-reader semantics, phone, tablet, iPhone, and iPad support.
- No account, ads, analytics, tracking SDK, or background location. Coordinates
  are reduced to kilometre-scale before storage or transmission.

## Suggested seasonal angle

Pitch the major update around safe outdoor planning during periods of heat, high
UV, storms, or poor air quality. Lead with “Tomorrow, in terms you remember,” then
show: familiar comparison, Best Time Outside, 10-day/AQI context, widgets and
watches, and the privacy/localization story.

## Evidence required before nomination

- Release-signed Android phone/tablet and Wear OS physical-device pass.
- TestFlight iPhone/iPad and paired physical Apple Watch pass, including VoiceOver.
- Android vitals and App Store crash/hang metrics after staged rollout.
- Five to eight narrative store screenshots per primary locale, a short captioned
  demo, press-kit artwork, support/privacy landing pages, and a direct editorial
  contact.
- Consent-free proof of value: public reviews, opt-in user stories, organic search
  growth, and store-console retention/engagement aggregates.

Simulator testing is sufficient for development and most layout/data-flow checks.
It is not evidence for final battery, Bluetooth handoff, complication refresh,
notifications, VoiceOver audio/gestures, or device-specific performance.
